What is the APA 7th Edition Approach to Digital Sources?

The core philosophy of the seventh edition of the APA Style is simple: provide enough information so that a reader can locate the source. When dealing with digital media, the priority is on identifying the author (or screen name), the date of the post, a descriptive title or snippet, and the platform name, followed by the URL.

How to Manage Complex Multi-Author References

Modern digital collaborations often involve numerous contributors. APA 7 has streamlined the way we handle these. For sources with up to 20 authors, you must list all names in the reference list. For works with 21 or more authors, include the first 19 names, followed by an ellipsis, and then the final author’s name.

When managing these lengthy citation lists, students often encounter errors in spacing or punctuation. Common apa 7 citation formatting pitfalls include:

  • Failing to use the & symbol before the final author in the reference list.
  • Italicising the entire title of a web page rather than just the container or site.
  • Miscalculating the "hanging indent" in Microsoft Word or Google Docs.
  • Forgetting to include retrieval dates only when the content is designed to change (like a live dashboard).

Citing Social Media in Academic Papers

Social media has become a primary source for qualitative research, yet it requires precise formatting to maintain academic credibility. Whether you are quoting a post on X (formerly Twitter) or a video on TikTok, the structure remains consistent:

  1. Author Name: Use the real name if available, or the handle in square brackets.
  2. Date: Year, Month Day.
  3. Content: Provide the first 20 words of the post as the "title."
  4. Platform: Identify the site (e.g., [Post], [Video], [Tweet]).
  5. URL: Direct link to the specific post.

Citing Generative AI Outputs Ethically

With the rise of large language models, many students ask how to cite these tools. APA recommends citing generative AI only if you have used it to generate specific content within your work. It is crucial to remember that AI is a tool, not an author. You should provide a reference that notes the model version and the company, but always supplement this with an appendix containing the full prompt and output.

Common APA 7 Citation Formatting Pitfalls to Avoid

Even seasoned researchers fall into traps when formatting digital sources. The most frequent issue is the confusion between "Author" and "Site Name." If a website has no clear author, use the organisation name. If the organisation name is the same as the site name, you omit the site name in the reference entry to avoid redundancy.

Another pitfall is the misuse of "Retrieved from." In APA 7, you do not need to include "Retrieved from" unless you are citing a source that is designed to change over time, such as a Wikipedia page or a live database. For static web pages, simply provide the URL at the end of the entry.
