How to Setup Your Dissertation Workflow

To establish a seamless connection, you need to rely on the "RTF Scan" method or the Zotero plugins designed for Scrivener. Follow these steps to optimize your setup:

  • Install the Better BibTeX Plugin: This Zotero add-on is essential for consistent cite-key generation.
  • Configure Scrivener’s Export: Set your project to export using Rich Text Format (.rtf) or OpenDocument (.odt) to ensure compatibility with citation processors.
  • The "Placeholder" Strategy: Insert placeholders (e.g., {Author, Year}) directly into your Scrivener draft.
  • Final Conversion: Once the draft is exported, use Zotero’s "RTF Scan" feature to automatically replace placeholders with your preferred citation style (APA 7, Chicago, etc.).
