What is the APA 7 Rule for Footnotes?

The APA 7th edition manual is quite specific: footnotes should not be used for citation purposes. In APA, you must use in-text parenthetical or narrative citations to credit sources. If you use footnotes to list references, you are essentially following the wrong style guide.

However, APA does permit two specific types of footnotes:

  • Content footnotes: These provide supplemental information that would be distracting if placed in the main body text.
  • Copyright footnotes: These are required when you reprint a long quotation or a copyrighted instrument (like a scale or survey) that requires permission.

How to Correctly Format Footnotes in APA 7

When you determine that a footnote is necessary, the formatting must be precise to meet university standards. Follow these steps to format your footnotes:

  1. Superscript numbers: Place the footnote number in the text immediately after the punctuation mark (e.g., end of the sentence).
  2. Placement: Footnotes should appear at the bottom of the page on which they are cited.
  3. Spacing: Use standard double-spacing for the footnote text, just like the rest of your document.
  4. Indentation: Indent the first line of the footnote like a standard paragraph.

The Great Debate: Footnotes vs. Endnotes

A common question among postgraduate students is whether endnotes are an acceptable alternative. In professional academic writing under APA 7, endnotes are generally discouraged.

Most universities in Australia expect all supplemental notes to be placed at the bottom of the page (footnotes). Moving them to the end of the paper (endnotes) forces the reader to flip back and forth, which breaks the flow of your argument. Student Formatting Checklist for APA 7

To avoid common formatting pitfalls, use this checklist before submitting your work:

  • Are all sources cited in the text using author-date formatting?
  • Have I removed all citations from my footnotes?
  • Is every footnote truly necessary for the reader's understanding?
  • Are my footnotes double-spaced?
  • Are the superscript numbers positioned correctly after the punctuation?
  • Is my reference list alphabetised and formatted with hanging indents?
