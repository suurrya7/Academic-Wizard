What is the most frequent APA 7 in-text error?

The most common oversight involves the inconsistent application of the "et al." rule. Under APA 7th edition, the rules for shortening multi-author citations have been simplified, yet many students continue to apply outdated 6th-edition logic. Using "et al." incorrectly can lead to academic integrity concerns regarding source attribution.

To master this, remember the primary rule: for a work with three or more authors, cite the name of the first author followed by "et al." from the very first citation. Do not list all authors unless you are dealing with a specific ambiguity issue where two different sources would collapse into the same abbreviated citation.

How to cite multiple authors in APA 7

Learning how to cite multiple authors in APA 7 requires precision. Whether you are using a narrative citation or a parenthetical one, the logic remains consistent. Here is a quick reference guide:

  • One author: (Smith, 2023) or Smith (2023).
  • Two authors: (Smith & Jones, 2023) or Smith and Jones (2023).
  • Three or more authors: (Smith et al., 2023) or Smith et al. (2023).

Ensure you never use an ampersand (&) inside a narrative sentence. Always use the word "and." Reserve the ampersand exclusively for parenthetical citations.

Fixing APA citation formatting loops

One of the most persistent frustrations for students is the "formatting loop"—where your word processor or citation manager keeps reverting your corrections to an incorrect state. Often, this happens because metadata in your library is corrupted, or the document’s automatic field codes are conflicting with manual overrides.

When fixing APA citation formatting loops, start by checking the source metadata in your software rather than the document itself. Avoiding citation errors in long papers

In a 50,000-word thesis, errors are inevitable if you do not have a robust system. To maintain accuracy, adopt these strategies:

  • Perform a "Find" search: Regularly search for common errors like "et al" (without the period) or inconsistent capitalisation in titles.
  • Centralise your library: Avoid manually typing citations; ensure your reference manager is synced and updated.
  • Check for phantom references: Ensure every citation in the text has a corresponding entry in the reference list, and vice versa.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Should I include the page number for all APA in-text citations?

No. You are only required to include page numbers when directly quoting a source. If you are paraphrasing or summarising an author’s work, page numbers are optional, though encouraged if it helps the reader locate a specific passage in a long document.

How do I handle "personal communications" in APA 7?

Personal communications—such as interviews, emails, or private messages—are cited in the text but are not included in the reference list. Use the format (J. Smith, personal communication, 12 January 2024).

What is the difference between a narrative and parenthetical citation?

A narrative citation incorporates the author into the flow of your sentence (e.g., "Brown (2022) argued that..."), whereas a parenthetical citation places the information at the end of the sentence within brackets (e.g., "...as argued by others (Brown, 2022).").

Can I use "et al." for a source with only two authors?

No. Never use "et al." for two authors. You must include both names in every citation, regardless of how many times you reference them throughout your paper.

How do I fix errors when my reference manager fails?

If your reference manager software is failing to produce correct formatting, first check for updates to the software or the style plugin. If issues persist, consider "unlinking" the fields once the paper is finished to manually fix any remaining discrepancies in the final draft.
