What is the general philosophy behind APA 7 digital referencing?

The APA 7th edition prioritises the ability of the reader to locate the exact source you have utilised. For digital content, this means providing a direct URL or DOI whenever possible. The goal is to balance brevity with traceability.

When you encounter complex media types, focus on the "who, when, what, and where" formula:

  • Author: Who created the content? (Include handles if personal names are unavailable).
  • Date: When was it posted or recorded?
  • Title: What is the specific name of the post, episode, or interactive module?
  • Source/Site: Where can it be found (e.g., Spotify, X, YouTube)?

How to handle podcast citations

Podcasts have become a staple in academic discourse. When citing podcasts in apa 7, you must distinguish between the series host/producer and the specific episode. The format is:

Host, A. A. (Host). (Year, Month Day). Title of episode (No. number) [Audio podcast episode]. In Title of podcast. Production Company. URL

Keep in mind that if the podcast is a standalone series, you simply treat the podcast title as the work’s title. Always prioritise the most stable link available, such as the official webpage of the podcast episode rather than a third-party aggregator.

Mastering citation guidelines for online video

Whether you are using a TED Talk or an educational YouTube video, the citation guidelines for online video require the author of the video (often the channel name) and the specific date. Note that for YouTube, you should include the real name of the user if it is known, followed by the screen name in square brackets.

For a video, the reference should look like this:

Author, A. A. [Screen name]. (Year, Month Day). Title of video [Video]. Site Name. URL

Always verify the publication date carefully. Sometimes re-uploads can complicate your research; cite the version you actually watched.

APA 7 referencing interactive media

Interactive media—such as educational apps, data visualisation tools, or online simulations—are becoming increasingly common in Australian research. Apa 7 referencing interactive media requires you to identify the developer or the organisation responsible for the content.

If the interactive media is a dynamic website, use the following structure:

Author, A. A. (Year). Title of the interactive work [Interactive media]. Site Name. URL

If the content is constantly updated, remember to include a "retrieved from" date if the content is designed to change over time. Being diligent with these details helps maintain the integrity of your research process. APA 7 digital source formatting: Social media

Social media posts are often transient. To cite a post correctly, you must capture the post content (up to the first 20 words) as the title. This allows the reader to identify the specific snippet you are discussing.

  • Twitter/X: Treat as a post. Include the handle in brackets.
  • Instagram: Use the caption as the title.
  • Facebook: Be sure to distinguish between a personal post and a page post.

Social media can be a minefield for academic tone. Always ensure you are selecting sources that align with the rigour of your argument.
