What is AI Scaffolding in Research?

AI scaffolding refers to using artificial intelligence as a supportive framework for your thinking process rather than a generator of finished prose. In an academic context, "ethical AI for literature review" means leveraging machine intelligence for structural tasks—such as categorising themes, managing bibliographies, or outlining arguments—while retaining full authorship of the synthesis and critique.

Think of the AI as a research assistant, not a ghostwriter. When you use AI to organise your sources, you are essentially offloading the "clerical" heavy lifting so you can focus on the high-level intellectual work that defines a strong thesis.

How to Structure a Literature Review Using AI Scaffolding

To build a logical narrative, you must first move beyond a simple summary of "Author A said this, Author B said that." Instead, you need a thematic approach. Here is how you can use AI to build that structure:

1. Extracting Thematic Threads

Upload your collection of PDFs to a secure, private AI interface. Ask the tool to "Identify the recurring themes and methodological gaps across these 10 papers." This provides you with a bird’s-eye view of the discourse.

2. Creating a Conceptual Map

Once you have identified the themes, use the AI to generate a structural outline. A prompt like, "Create a logical argument structure for a literature review on [Topic] that progresses from historical context to current debates," can help you visualise the flow of your writing.

3. Implementing Synthesis Techniques

Once you have an outline, focus on literature review synthesis techniques. Do not ask the AI to write the paragraphs for you. Instead, ask it to "Compare and contrast the findings of these three specific articles regarding [Specific Variable]." Using this method ensures you are synthesising, not just summarising.
