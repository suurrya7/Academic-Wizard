What is the core issue with multi-author citations?

The most common frustration students face involves the transition from three or more authors to the "et al." shorthand. Under the current APA 7 rules, simplicity is the objective. If you have a source with three or more authors, you truncate the in-text citation from the very first mention. It is a departure from older editions that required you to list every name once before switching to the shorthand.

How to format complex digital sources correctly

Digital sources often provide incomplete metadata, leading to the dreaded "citation loop" where you cannot find a clear author or date. APA 7 prioritises clarity and retrievability. If a digital source lacks a conventional author, use the organisation or site name. If that is absent, move the title to the author position.

APA 7 reference list rules: The essential checklist

Your reference list is the final gatekeeper of your academic rigour. To avoid common pitfalls, follow this hierarchy for every entry:

  • Author: Who created the content? (Individuals or organisations).
  • Date: When was it published? Use (n.d.) if no date is available.
  • Title: Use italics for stand-alone works like books or reports.
  • Source: Provide the retrieval link or publisher details.

Always ensure your hanging indent is set correctly in your word processor. A disorganised list can signal to a marker that you have rushed the final presentation of your thesis, regardless of how strong your analysis actually is.

How to handle sources with no identifiable author

When you encounter a web page that provides valuable data but lacks a specific author, do not panic. APA 7 allows for corporate authorship. If the content is produced by a government body or a registered charity, credit them as the author. If no such body exists, begin the entry with the title of the article or web page.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Below are common queries regarding citation management and APA 7 troubleshooting.

Do I need to list all authors in the reference list if there are more than 20?

No. Under APA 7th edition rules, for sources with 21 or more authors, list the first 19, then insert an ellipsis (three dots), and conclude with the name of the final author. Do not include an ampersand before the final author.

What should I do if a digital source has no date?

If a source has no date of publication or update, use "n.d." (which stands for "no date") in the parentheses. This applies to both the in-text citation and the full entry in your reference list.

Can I use "et al." for a source with only two authors?

No. You should always include both author surnames in every in-text citation for a work with only two authors. The "et al." shorthand is reserved exclusively for works with three or more authors.

Is it acceptable to shorten titles in the reference list?

Generally, no. Titles should be provided in full as they appear on the work itself. However, you should use sentence case for the titles of articles, chapters, and books, while keeping the first word of the title and subtitle capitalised.
