How to Optimise Mendeley for Collaborative Research

Mendeley, now owned by Elsevier, provides a more "all-in-one" experience. It shines in its integrated PDF annotation tools and its social discovery features. If your research involves a large lab group or shared project folders, Mendeley’s cloud-based syncing is often perceived as more user-friendly for teams.

When to choose Mendeley:

  • You prefer an integrated PDF reader and annotation workspace.
  • Your research group already uses Mendeley’s shared folders.
  • You value a sleek, modern UI that is updated frequently by a corporate entity.

Comparing Features: Zotero vs. Mendeley

When selecting your tool, consider these critical factors for 2026:

  1. Export Capabilities: How easily can you move your library if you change software? Zotero’s open format (RIS/BibTeX) is generally superior to Mendeley’s proprietary handling.
  2. Browser Extensions: Zotero’s "one-click" save is arguably the most efficient in the industry for capturing web-based citations.
  3. Performance: Mendeley can become sluggish when managing libraries exceeding 5,000 sources. Zotero remains consistently responsive regardless of library size.
  4. Cost: Both offer free versions, but Mendeley restricts cloud storage more aggressively as you scale your research.

Ultimately, for a long-form thesis, Zotero is generally considered the more reliable choice for Australian students who prioritise control and longevity over "out-of-the-box" social features. It acts as a permanent repository for your academic career, rather than just a tool for your current degree.
