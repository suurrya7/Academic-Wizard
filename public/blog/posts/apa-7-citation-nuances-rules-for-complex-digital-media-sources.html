What is the APA 7th Edition Approach to Digital Media?

The APA style guidelines prioritize transparency and recoverability. When citing digital media, you are providing a roadmap for your reader to find the exact source you referenced. Unlike traditional books, digital media often lacks clear authorship or standard publication dates. APA 7 solves this by emphasizing the "who, when, what, and where" format.

How to Approach Social Media Citations

Citing social media in research requires a balance between the platform’s handle and the user's real name. If both are available, provide both to ensure the source is easily trackable. APA 7 mandates that you include the first 20 words of the post as the "title" to help your reader understand the content of the cited media.

  • Author: The individual or organizational name.
  • Handle: The unique identifier, usually in brackets.
  • Date: The exact date of the post (Year, Month Day).
  • Content: The first 20 words of the post.
  • Source: The platform name and the URL.

Fixing APA 7 In-Text Citations for Complex Media

One of the most frequent errors students make involves fixing APA 7 in-text citations when the media has a group author or a non-standard handle. Remember, if a social media post does not have a clear individual author, use the organization as the group author.

When dealing with complex multi-author citations, always adhere to the "et al." rule. For sources with three or more authors, truncate the citation to the first author's surname followed by "et al." right from the first mention. This keeps your narrative flow clean and professional.

Referencing Podcasts and Multimodal Content

Podcasts have become a staple in academic discourse. In APA 7, a podcast citation is treated similarly to a journal article but replaces the journal title with the name of the podcast and the episode number. If you are struggling with the structure of these citations, ensure you are not conflating the show name with the episode title.

APA 7 Reference List Formatting: Best Practices

The reference list is your final safety net. Every entry must be double-spaced and include a hanging indent. For digital media, the URL must be a live link. However, do not include a period after the URL, as this can break the hyperlink and prevent your reader from accessing the source.

Consistency is key. If you are citing a YouTube video, include the name of the uploader as the author, even if they are not the content creator. This ensures that the digital trail remains unbroken.
