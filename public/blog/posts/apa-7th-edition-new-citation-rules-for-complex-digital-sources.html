What is the APA 7th Edition Approach to Digital Media?

The core philosophy of APA 7 is flexibility coupled with consistency. Unlike older iterations that forced digital sources into rigid templates, the 7th edition emphasizes the "Who, When, What, and Where" structure:

  • Who: The author or screen name.
  • When: The exact date of posting.
  • What: The title of the post or the content description (in italics).
  • Where: The site name and the URL or DOI.

How to Handle Social Media Citations

Citing social media in APA 7 is a common point of confusion. Whether it is an X (formerly Twitter) post, an Instagram caption, or a LinkedIn article, the format remains consistent to ensure the reader knows exactly what they are looking at.

For social media posts:

  1. Author Name: Provide the real name followed by the handle in square brackets.
  2. Date: Include the year, month, and day.
  3. Content: Provide the first 20 words of the post (if it’s a text post).
  4. Platform: Indicate the site name.
  5. URL: Provide the direct link to the specific post.

Example: Surname, F. [@handle]. (2026, January 15). The first 20 words of the post go here and are italicized [Status update]. Site Name. URL
