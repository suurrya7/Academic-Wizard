What is Ethical Prompt Engineering for Research?

Ethical prompt engineering involves crafting specific, structured instructions for LLMs (Large Language Models) to assist in cognitive processes without outsourcing the creative or analytical heavy lifting. It is about using AI as a sounding board, an organisational assistant, or a research navigator, rather than a writer.

Responsible AI practices for students require transparency. If you use a tool to refine your methodology or brainstorm, you must document it in accordance with your university’s specific guidelines. Never input sensitive or unpublished data into public LLMs to ensure you protect your intellectual property.

How to Use AI for Literature Review Outlining

A literature review can be overwhelming. AI is excellent for identifying patterns in large volumes of text. Here is a framework for using LLMs for research brainstorming without crossing the line into academic misconduct:

  • Synthesis Assistance: Ask the AI to identify common themes across three provided abstracts rather than asking it to write your review.
  • Structuring: Use prompts like, "Propose a logical outline for a 2,000-word literature review on the impact of climate policy on Australian agriculture, focusing on three major economic models."
  • Gap Identification: Prompt the AI to identify potential counter-arguments or research gaps in your preliminary thesis statement to strengthen your critical analysis.

How to Verify AI Use: A Step-by-Step Guide

When you incorporate AI into your workflow, maintain a "Human-in-the-Loop" policy. Use these steps to remain ethical:

  1. Input Human Thought: Start with your own research questions. Only use AI to refine or expand on your existing ideas.
  2. Verify Citations: Never accept AI-generated references. Manually search for the source to confirm its existence and content.
  3. Documentation: Keep a log of your prompts. If your university requires an AI disclosure statement, you will have a clear record of how the tool was used.
  4. Self-Correction: Treat the AI output as a draft that requires significant refinement. If the text sounds too robotic, you need to rewrite it to reflect your personal understanding.
