## 2. Key Actionable Opportunities

1. **Title Tag Truncation Fixes (Quick Win):** Programmatic subject page title templates frequently append long location strings that exceed Google's 60-character / 600-pixel threshold. Shortening them to `[Subject] [Service] [Country] | Academic Wizard` immediately restores full visibility in SERP snippets.
2. **Meta Description CTA Standardization:** Commercial descriptions currently average 168–182 characters. Trimming them to 145–155 characters ensures the concluding call-to-action (`"Turnitin-safe. WhatsApp 24/7!"`) is never cut off on mobile screens.
