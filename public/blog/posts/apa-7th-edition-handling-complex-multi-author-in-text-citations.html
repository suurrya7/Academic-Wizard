What is the APA 7th Edition Rule for Multi-Author Citations?

The core shift in the APA 7th edition was a move toward brevity and consistency. For sources with three or more authors, you no longer need to list every name in the first citation. Instead, the focus is on providing enough information for the reader to identify the source in your reference list without cluttering your narrative.

Following the correct apa 7 citation rules for four or more authors ensures that your writing remains professional and uncluttered. Whether you are drafting an undergraduate essay or a PhD thesis, the rule remains consistent across all document types.

How to Use "et al." Correctly

The term "et al." is an abbreviation of the Latin et alii, meaning "and others." In APA 7th edition, the rule is straightforward:

  • Sources with one or two authors: Always list both authors every time the work is cited in the text.
  • Sources with three or more authors: Use the surname of the first author followed by "et al." from the very first citation onwards.

For example, if you are referencing a study by Smith, Jones, Brown, and Wilson, your in-text citation should look like: (Smith et al., 2024).

Handling Common Complex Scenarios

Sometimes, your research involves multiple sources that, when abbreviated, end up looking exactly the same. This is a common point of confusion when following an academic writing style guide.

What to do if "et al." citations become identical

If you have two different sources that, after adding "et al.", appear identical, you must differentiate them to avoid confusing your reader:

  1. List as many authors as needed to distinguish the two sources.
  2. Include the remaining author names until the citations are distinct.
  3. If the sources have the same number of authors, add as many names as necessary to create differentiation.

For example, if you have (Johnson, Smith, et al., 2023) and (Johnson, Miller, et al., 2023), these are already distinct. However, if the authors are (Johnson, Smith, Miller, et al., 2023) and (Johnson, Smith, Brown, et al., 2023), you must continue listing names until the distinction is clear.

APA Formatting Cheat Sheet 2026: Key Takeaways

To keep your work clean, use this apa formatting cheat sheet 2026 for quick reference:

  • Parenthetical Citations: Use an ampersand (&) between the last two authors if you are listing them, though for 3+ authors, use the "et al." format.
  • Narrative Citations: Use the word "and" between the first author and "et al." if referring to them in the flow of a sentence (e.g., "According to Smith et al. (2024)...").
  • Punctuation: Ensure there is a full stop after "al." but not after "et" because "et" is not an abbreviation.

Correcting Bibliography Errors

Often, students focus so much on the in-text citation that they neglect the reference list. The most common error is failing to list all authors in the bibliography. While in-text citations for 3+ authors use "et al.", the reference list entry for APA 7th edition must include up to 20 authors. If a source has 21 or more authors, list the first 19, insert an ellipsis, and add the final author's name.
