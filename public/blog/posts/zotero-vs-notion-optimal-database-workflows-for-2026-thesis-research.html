The modern research workflow relies on two pillars: Zotero for rigorous source management and Notion for synthetic knowledge organisation. Zotero serves as your "source of truth," housing your PDFs, metadata, and automated citations. Notion, meanwhile, acts as your "thinking space," where you map themes, draft arguments, and synthesise complex literature.

By connecting these two, you create a seamless loop. When you save a paper using the Zotero browser extension, the metadata is pulled automatically. How to set up your Zotero and Notion workflow

Creating a synchronised system requires a few deliberate steps to ensure long-term stability. Avoid the common mistake of duplicating your work. Instead, leverage automation to keep your databases in sync.

  • Centralise your library: Install the Zotero desktop application and the browser connector. Ensure all PDFs are renamed using a consistent naming convention (e.g., Year_Author_ShortTitle).
  • Establish a sync bridge: Use a tool like Notion2Zotero or Make.com to create an automated workflow that pushes new Zotero items directly into your Notion research dashboard.
  • Standardise your tagging: Use Zotero’s tagging system to categorise sources by research theme. These tags will automatically appear in Notion, allowing you to filter your literature by core academic arguments.
  • Master your bibliography: While Notion is great for synthesis, always return to your primary reference manager for final output.
