Why Does Original Writing Get Flagged?

AI detectors work by measuring "burstiness" (variations in sentence structure) and "perplexity" (the complexity of word choice). Ironically, highly structured, formulaic academic writing—the kind often taught in introductory university courses—shares these same traits with AI models. If you rely heavily on standard academic transitions or predictable paragraph structures, you may inadvertently trigger Turnitin AI Detection: Why Your Original Writing is Being Flagged.

The goal isn't to "trick" the system, but to cultivate a robust, unique voice that reflects deep research and critical thought. By focusing on your own perspective, you move toward human-authored content verification, proving that the insights on the page belong to you.

How to Audit Your Own Tone for Authenticity

Auditing your work requires a shift from passive proofreading to active self-critique. Use this checklist to strengthen your academic voice and ensure your writing feels distinctly human:

  • Vary your sentence syntax: Mix short, punchy analytical statements with longer, compound sentences that explain complex concepts.
  • Embed personal analysis: Don't just summarise sources. Add a layer of "I argue" or "This perspective contradicts..." to show you are actively engaging with the literature.
  • Use specific, concrete examples: AI often deals in generalisations. Use unique case studies, specific data points, or nuanced local Australian examples that a generic model would be unlikely to synthesise.
  • Check for flow: Read your draft aloud. If it sounds like a lecture or a generic summary, add transition phrases that reflect your own critical path.

Improving Academic Voice Through Narrative

One of the most effective ways to avoid avoiding AI writing style flags is to integrate your own academic narrative. In an essay, your voice is the thread that connects disparate sources. When you are struggling to find this balance, it is helpful to look at how your tone shifts across sections.

Ethical Writing Practices for the Modern Student

True ethical writing practices are built on transparency. Using generative AI for ideation or outlining is generally acceptable if your university allows it, but it must be clearly disclosed. However, the heavy lifting of synthesis, critical analysis, and original argumentation must remain entirely your own.
