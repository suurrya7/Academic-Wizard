How to Approach APA 7 Citing Multimedia Sources

The core principle of APA 7th edition is consistency. Regardless of the format—be it an audio podcast or a video documentary—you must provide the author (or creator) and the date. If you are struggling with manual formatting, using a reliable APA-compliant citation generator can save you significant time while minimizing the risk of a minor formatting penalty.

When citing digital media in-text, follow these standard patterns:

  • Parenthetical Citation: (Creator Surname, Year).
  • Narrative Citation: Creator Surname (Year) argued that...
  • Specific Locations: If the content has a timestamp, use it in place of page numbers (e.g., 14:22).

Formatting Digital Media References: The Essentials

When you transition from in-text citations to your reference list, the rules become more granular. Here is how you should handle common digital formats:

1. Podcasts and Audio Media

List the host or executive producer as the author. Include the series title in italics, followed by the specific episode title. Always provide the URL at the end of the reference entry.

2. Social Media Posts

If the post is a thread, cite the individual post. Use the user's real name and their handle in brackets. The date should be as specific as possible, including the day and month.

3. YouTube and Video Lectures

The creator of the channel is typically considered the author. If a specific person is speaking, you may list them as the primary author if they are the primary source of the information. Ensure the video title is italicized and include the platform name.

Complex Multi-Author Citation Rules

Digital media often involves collaboration, such as group podcasts or multi-authored webinars. APA 7 has streamlined these rules significantly. For three or more authors, use the first author’s name followed by "et al." right from the first in-text citation. Never list all authors in the parenthetical citation unless the group is very small or it is required by your specific department's style guide.
