What are the core APA 7 multi-author rules?

The seventh edition of the APA Style guide simplified many legacy requirements, but multi-author sources remain a focus for markers. Understanding the distinction between parenthetical and narrative citations is the first step toward flawless referencing.

  • One author: (Surname, Year).
  • Two authors: (Surname & Surname, Year). Always use the ampersand (&) in parenthetical citations.
  • Three or more authors: Use the first author’s surname followed by "et al." for every citation, including the first one.

When you encounter complex authorship in digital media, such as a podcast with multiple hosts or a collaborative YouTube video, the "et al." rule is your best friend. It keeps your writing clean and shifts the focus back to your analysis rather than the formatting of the bibliography.

How to handle complex APA 7 citation formats for digital media

Digital media often lacks clear attribution. When citing podcasts, webinars, or video essays, determine if you are citing the individual speaker or the producing entity as the author. Citing podcasts in APA 7: A practical approach

Podcasts are increasingly common in contemporary research. Because they often feature multiple contributors, students frequently trip up on whether to list the host or the executive producer. APA 7 guidelines specify that the host acts as the author.

For a podcast with multiple hosts, list them in the same order as they appear in the source. If there are three or more, truncate to "et al." immediately.

Example of a parenthetical citation:

(Smith, Jones, & Brown, 2024)

Example of a narrative citation:

Smith et al. (2024) argue that digital media literacy remains a core challenge in the Australian curriculum.

Navigating authorship in collaborative digital works

Modern digital content is rarely the work of a single individual. Video essays, for instance, may involve editors, writers, and producers. In APA 7, stick to the primary creator. If no individual name is available, use the name of the group or organisation that owns the account.

Remember that the "et al." abbreviation is Latin for et alii (and others). It is never italicised in the APA 7 system. Precision here demonstrates that you have engaged with the official style guide, rather than relying on outdated templates.
